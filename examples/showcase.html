Summary

Revenue reached 3.4 million dollars, 15% more than in the first quarter and ahead of plan. Growth came from the north, where a new store opened, and from online orders in the west1. Costs stayed within budget for the third quarter in a row.

  • Operating margin: 18%, +2 points
  • Online share of sales: 21%

Growth

Quarterly growth is measured as the change of revenue against the previous quarter, where R1 and R2 are the revenues of the two quarters:

g=R2-R1R1×100%g=\frac{R_{2}-R_{1}}{R_{1}}\times 100\%

Figure 1. Revenue in April, May and June, thousand dollars: last year (light) and this year (dark)

Every month of the quarter beat the same month of last year. June was the strongest month on record2; the summer sale moved part of the July demand into it, so July will look weaker in comparison.
